    Install free

    Live in about 20 minutes.

    You need a domain (like yourcompany.com) on a free Cloudflare account.

    1. Deploy

      Click Deploy to Cloudflare and follow the prompts. Keep the Worker name mailos.

    2. Create your admin

      Open your new app at /setup and create the admin account.

    3. Add your domain

      Connect your domain, choose Resend's free tier for sending, and create your mailboxes.
